User direction, saved September 25, 2026. Apply to all future DLL Studio video work.

1. **Every video must contain at least one established DLL Studio character:** Luca, Leo, Vienna, Bianna, Doo Wop Dog, or Gramps. The main character of this adventure is an existing DLL Studio character: **Leo**. Captain Giggle is Leo’s adventure role, not a separate human hero. Preserve approved character designs.
2. **Deliver a continuous standard episode.** It needs a coherent beginning, developing story and ending, with continuous character, location, voice, music and prop continuity. Do not present a sequence of disconnected three-minute videos as the final episode.
3. Three-minute pieces are internal production segments. Once complete, assemble them into **one long-form video for social media and YouTube**. Include one opening and one closing; remove repeated intros, recaps, segment titles, end cards, fade-outs and silence at internal joins.
4. Every segment must itself include an established DLL Studio character. Keep handles available for editing, and review both sides of every join for continuity. Do not insert artificial gaps to reach an exact segment duration.
5. The current episode targets **10 minutes**: three three-minute segments and a final one-minute segment. Scene timing is subordinate to natural storytelling during final audio/edit review. Export a continuous 16:9 1920×1080 master, with captions and consistent audio. Additional aspect-ratio versions must preserve the episode’s story and framing; platform-specific encoding limits need checking at release.
6. Optional interactive choices belong in the companion. The linear episode must continue on a planned path without clicks and without implying it can hear answers.
7. Clearly distinguish scripts, generated artwork, animatics, rendered segments and a finished episode. Never claim an MP4 exists until it has actually been rendered and verified. Do not upload or publish without authorization.
