## 2. Synopsis

Bubbleville’s Friendship Festival is nearly ready when a giant crocodile in a floppy yellow hat appears. Captain Giggle and his friends discover that Snapper was accidentally left off the invitation route. A mistaken cake invitation, an unreliable cupcake gadget and a windy decorating mishap send the team on a comic adventure. Viewers help choose approaches and routes, find clues and name a feeling. By listening, setting kind boundaries, returning the cake and combining their abilities, the friends turn exclusion into a welcoming parade. Snapper becomes a helper, the town apologizes for judging him, and everyone celebrates with an original song.

## 4. Full 20-scene script

Each scene is exactly 60 seconds in the edit. Scenes 4 and 17 use the requested eight-second answer windows, preceded by two seconds of visual preview; other response windows are ten seconds. Six 10-second beats specify action, all spoken words, essential sounds and overlays. Spoken lines finish within their beat; use the remaining time for acting, not faster speech. Timings are an animatic target, not a verified recording. The response windows are included in the duration. Choice scenes have an eight- or ten-second hold followed by a ten-second variant; never append a variant to the master. The linear cut uses A in scene 4, B in scene 9 and A first in scene 14. It explains “Point or say your choice; our story will try one way.” It must not claim YouTube can read an answer.

Before Scene 19, the closing narrator line in Scene 18 may be delivered over its last four seconds as: “You may clap softly twice and wave, or just listen.” This replaces, rather than adds to, its invitation line when recording.
